# User Guide

## 1. Login

Enter an email or username and password. Accounts are created by an Admin.

## 2. Change Password

If an account has `mustChangePassword`, the app opens the password-change screen after login. Users can also change their password from Account & System.

## 3. Admin Dashboard

Review sales, inventory, procurement, predictive planning, and recent audit activity.

## 4. Categories

Admins create, edit, archive, and restore category records. Staff can view categories as product reference data.

## 5. Products

Admins manage product SKU, name, category, price, cost, status, unit type, and reorder point. Staff can view product records for sales reference.

## 6. Suppliers

Admins manage supplier profile, contact, address, status, and notes.

## 7. Inventory

Admins view current stock, stock health, reorder point status, and recommended reorder quantities. Admins can create stock adjustments.

## 8. Stock Movements

Admins review append-only inventory movements from adjustments, sales, and purchase receipts.

## 9. Staff POS

Staff select products, enter quantities, choose payment method, and complete sales. Cash sales require cash received and show change due.

## 10. Sales History

Admin and Staff users can review completed sales and line details.

## 11. Predictive Analysis

Admins select a product, choose a 7, 14, or 30-day Moving Average window, choose a 7, 14, or 30-day forecast horizon, and generate a product forecast from completed sales.

## 12. Forecast Evaluation

Admins compare matured forecast days with actual completed sales. The section shows MAE, WAPE, bias, totals, and daily predicted-vs-actual differences. WAPE is unavailable when evaluated actual demand totals zero.

## 13. Purchase Orders

Admins create draft or ordered purchase orders with supplier and product line items.

## 14. Receiving

Admins receive ordered purchase orders. Receiving creates purchase receipt movements and increases stock.

## 15. Reports

Admins review sales trend, top products, inventory summary, inventory attention, and predictive forecast summary.

## 16. User Management

Admins create Staff accounts, edit user profiles and roles, change statuses, and reset temporary passwords.

## 17. Audit Logs

Admins review data-change, admin-action, security, reporting, and system events. Login history is available in the same audit area.

## 18. Account & System

Admin and Staff users can view account/system context and change their password.

## 19. Logout

Use Log out from the application shell to remove the local session token.

## Role Access Summary

Admin: Dashboard, Products, Categories, Suppliers, Inventory, Stock Movements, Sales History, Predictive Analysis, Purchase Orders, Reports, User Management, Audit Logs, Account & System.

Staff: POS, Sales History, Products read-only, Categories read-only, Account & System.
